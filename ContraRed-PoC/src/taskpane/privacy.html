Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 17, 2026

ContraRed ("we", "us", "our") operates the ContraRed contract review platform, including the web dashboard and the Microsoft Word add-in. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash). If you use a paid plan, we collect billing details processed by our payment provider.

Contract Content: When you submit a contract for analysis, the text content is sent to our servers for AI-powered review. Contract text is encrypted at rest using AES-256 (Fernet) when field-level encryption is enabled.

Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with our service, including features used, analysis requests, timestamps, and error logs for debugging purposes.

Device Information: We collect your IP address, browser type, and operating system for security (rate limiting, fraud detection) and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the ContraRed service
  • To perform AI-powered contract analysis and generate redline suggestions
  • To authenticate your identity and manage your account
  • To enforce usage quotas and subscription tier limits
  • To communicate important service updates and security notices
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats

3. AI Processing and Third-Party Services

Contract text submitted for analysis is processed using Google Gemini AI (via Google Cloud Vertex AI). Your contract text is sent to Google's API solely for the purpose of generating analysis results. Google's data processing terms apply to this usage.

We also use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase (PostgreSQL database hosting) — stores account data and analysis results
  • Render — hosts our API backend
  • Netlify — hosts our web dashboard and Word add-in

We do not sell, rent, or share your contract content with third parties for marketing purposes. Contract text is not used to train AI models.

4. Data Retention

Account data is retained for as long as your account is active. Analysis results and document records are retained for 90 days after creation, unless you delete them earlier. When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed within 30 days.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • TLS/HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 field-level encryption for stored contract text
  • Salted password hashing (bcrypt)
  • Rate limiting on all authentication endpoints
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) for multi-user organizations
  • Audit logging for sensitive operations

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored in our system
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your analysis results and documents
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time by closing your account

For EU/EEA residents: You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority under the GDPR.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication (HttpOnly session cookies) and CSRF protection. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

8. Children's Privacy

ContraRed is not intended for use by individuals under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of material changes via email at least 14 days before they take effect.
